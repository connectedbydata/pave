## AI Use Statement

We have used Large Language Model AI tools in the following ways as part of this project:

### Research and presentation support

* Members of the research team may use LLMs are one element of information search and retrieval (for example, to identify where certain case study fields may be answered within project documents). All evidence is cross-checked against the original source before being entered into the Case Book. 

* When case studies are submitted by contributors, we ask whether AI tools were used to generate summary text, and whether these summaries have been checked for accuracy by a human. We cross-check against project documents to ensure details are accurate. 

* We created a small web service to generate consistent case summaries, and to help check that key data fields are populated accurately. Two options for generated summaries are presented to editors in the case editing interface, and may be copied and edited into the case description. 

Only information already in the public domain is fed into LLMs for this.
