recruitment-description: 'Recruitment varied across the different phases of tool development.
  For the initial English and Spanish models, we organized an open online event and
  invited potential participants drawing on a list of feminicide monitoring projects
  originally compiled by Feminicidio Uruguay (one of the project partners) and expanded
  through further research by the Data + Feminism Lab, as well as on academic and
  activist networks built by the DCF team over time. For the Portuguese and Swahili
  iterations, recruitment drew on relationships built through the project''s own qualitative
  research: groups that had participated in interviews or informal conversations about
  their data practices were approached directly about collaborating to expand the
  tools into their language and context. Each iteration involved a distinct participatory
  process with local partners rather than a single uniform approach.'
