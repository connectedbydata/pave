# Recruitment description
recruitment-description: "With the aim of providing a space for laypeople across different\
  \ countries to explore and express their opinions on desirable AI development and\
  \ deployment, we decided on a mix of participant selection methods. The participant\
  \ outreach process employed targeted recruitment through various digital and analog\
  \ communication channels and engagement with communities (e.g., neighbourhood associations,\
  \ \u2026) that shared information about the dialogues. This approach aimed to enhance\
  \ diversity in gender, age, and AI knowledge, resulting in a self-selected pre-registered\
  \ set of the interested general population. We chose self-selection based on the\
  \ principle of universality, which reflects a participatory ideal of citizen engagement.\
  \ We implemented purposive sampling with a random component on all pre-registrations.\
  \ We ensured that each dialogue workshop group was gender-diverse and diverse across\
  \ age clusters (20-year intervals: \u2018low\u2019, \u2018middle\u2019, \u2018high\u2019\
  ) and AI knowledge levels (\u2018low\u2019, \u2018middle\u2019, \u2018high\u2019\
  ). We refer to the group of laypeople selected through this approach as a \u201C\
  mini-public,\u201D in which people gather to discuss matters of public interest."
