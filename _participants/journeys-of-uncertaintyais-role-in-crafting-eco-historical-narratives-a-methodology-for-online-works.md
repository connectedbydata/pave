# Additional notes
additional-notes: 'Additional notes


  The project generated approximately 30 AI-generated artworks that were released
  under a Creative Commons licence and can be freely shared and reused in educational,
  cultural and public engagement contexts. This open-materials approach is important
  because the case is not only a one-off workshop, but also a reusable method for
  AI engagement, Futures Literacy and uncertainty reflection.


  The engagement format has been further developed into a workbook, making the prompts,
  reflective exercises and creative process accessible beyond the original participant
  group. This supports adaptation by educators, cultural practitioners, researchers
  and civic innovation actors who want to engage non-expert publics with generative
  AI in a critical and creative way.


  International transfer is already being explored: the adaptation of the format for
  teaching contexts in Brazil and Russia is currently under discussion. This suggests
  that the case has potential for use across different cultural, educational and linguistic
  settings, while keeping the focus on bias, authorship, uncertainty, ecological imagination
  and responsible AI futures.
