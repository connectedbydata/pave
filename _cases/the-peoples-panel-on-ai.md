# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "The People\u2019s Panel on AI brought\
  \ together representative members of the public to attend, observe and discuss key\
  \ events at the AI Fringe, which was held alongside the UK Government\u2019s AI\
  \ Safety Summit at the beginning of November 2023.\n\nThrough a deliberative process\
  \ facilitated by Hopkins Van Mil the People\u2019s Panel on AI produced a public\
  \ report giving their verdict on AI and their recommendations to government, industry,\
  \ civil society and academia for further action."
# Project status
project-status: Complete
# Headline findings
headline-findings: "On 3rd November 2023, the People\u2019s Panel on AI made seven\
  \ recommendations based on their deliberation of discussions at the AI Fringe and\
  \ UK AI Safety Summit:\n\n1\\. A global governing body for AI to bring together\
  \ citizens, impartial experts and governments from across the world, and ensure\
  \ regulatory collaboration that includes the global south.\n\n2\\. A system of governance\
  \ for AI in the UK that places citizens at the heart of decision making drawing\
  \ on input from scientists, researchers, ethicists, civil society, academia and\
  \ industry to inform and provide evidence for government and citizens to then work\
  \ together on decisions.\n\n3\\. Awareness raising about AI across society. From\
  \ the classroom to the home. From the workplace to the community. Highlighting risks\
  \ such as addiction to social media, as well as the opportunities that AI offers.\n\
  \n4\\. A safe transition, with training, to support people into a world of work\
  \ alongside AI, ensuring no-one is left behind.\n\n5\\. A continued national conversation\
  \ on AI, including retaining the People\u2019s Panel to keep public voice live in\
  \ a fast-changing AI landscape. We citizens\ncan do jury service and as such are\
  \ already trusted to make life-impacting and significant decisions.\n\n6\\. Focus\
  \ on inclusive collaboration, to set out a vision of life where AI is used to enhance\
  \ and balance human needs.\n\n7\\. Stakeholders acting with transparency at all\
  \ times. An example of this might include a \u2018black box flight recorder\u2019\
  \ approach to AI models: protecting intellectual property but shared when things\
  \ go wrong.\n"
