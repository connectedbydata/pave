# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "The 2025 Citizen's Assembly on AI in\
  \ French Speaking Switzerland was organized in November 2025 by the EPFL AI Center\
  \ in collaboration with the Research Hub on Democratic Innovations at the University\
  \ of Geneva and Demoscan. \n\n\nThe project included a survey of 734 residents of\
  \ French-speaking Switzerland and a  deliberation process with 40 randomly selected\
  \ citizens of Romandie reflecting the demographics of the population, meeting over\
  \ a total of four days. \n\nIt resulted in 20 proposals that outline the foundations\
  \ of responsible AI governance, including proposals on privacy protection, transparency,\
  \ and meaningful human control.\n"
# What limitations to the project should be noted?
what-limitations-to-the-project-should-be-noted: "The project report notes a number\
  \ of methodological limitations that impacted the project. A low 10% response rate\
  \ on the initial survey due to tight deadlines and the technical nature of artificial\
  \ intelligence. Additionally, a notable \"volunteer effect\" emerged among those\
  \ willing to commit to the four-day, in-person format, creating an initial pool\
  \ that skewed toward tertiary-educated individuals, residents of the canton of Vaud,\
  \ and left-of-center political alignments. \n\nOrganizers used a weighted lottery\
  \ to correct these imbalances, the small size of the final assembly (40 participants)\
  \ meant that random dropouts and subsequent replacements still caused minor geographic\
  \ skews, such as an overrepresentation of the canton of Jura and an underrepresentation\
  \ of Neuch\xE2tel.  \n\nPractical constraints of the format restricted participation\
  \ to those available on weekends, and the entire project was structurally limited\
  \ to the French-speaking regions of Switzerland, meaning it did not capture the\
  \ perspectives of the rest of the country."

# Headline findings
headline-findings: "The Citizens Assembly component resulted in five issues, and two\
  \ objectives, and a set of supporting recommendations for society and policy makers.\
  \ \n\nThe issues and objectives are detailed bellow, and the more detailed recommendations\
  \ can found in the full report. \n\nISSUE 1: Determine the degree of state involvement\
  \ and responsibility in AI, in its development and implementation in Switzerland.\
  \  \n\nObjective 1: Adopt a national AI strategy.  \n\nObjective 2: Promote the\
  \ development of AI in accordance with human rights, enriched by Swiss values, in\
  \ order to shape the future of AI.  \n\nISSUE 2: Democratize access to AI and promote\
  \ equality, encourage healthy, responsible and ethical use, notably to limit dependency\
  \ and foster social interactions, including in the real world.  \n\nObjective 1:\
  \ Raise public awareness of AI, train people in its responsible use, and facilitate\
  \ access for everyone.  \n\nObjective 2: Promote social, cultural, sporting and\
  \ artistic gatherings so as not to fall into an \"all-AI\" world.  \n\nISSUE 3:\
  \ Prepare the world of work for the AI transition with regard to social and financial\
  \ protection and to technological changes in jobs.  \n\nObjective 1: Anticipate\
  \ the socio-professional impacts linked to the advent of AI.  \n\nObjective 2: Guarantee\
  \ dignity, and human and financial support, for people who may lose their job or\
  \ experience a change in their role due to AI.  \n\nISSUE 4: Guarantee the origin\
  \ and traceability of outputs and distinguish what comes from humans from what comes\
  \ from AI.  \n\nObjective 1: Label human creation.  \n\nObjective 2: Adapt and strengthen\
  \ copyright protection.  \n\nISSUE 5: Define responsible and ethical practices for\
  \ AI users (legal entities and natural persons) that guarantee data protection and\
  \ the reliability of tools shared online.  \n\nObjective 1: Define criteria for\
  \ good practices for users and providers and create instruments (national labeling\
  \ body, legislation, etc.) to implement and assess these good practices.  \n\nObjective\
  \ 2: Fight cybercrime. \n"

# Inclusion efforts
inclusion-efforts: "The project ensured accessibility and inclusion by using a weighted\
  \ random lottery (sortition) to select the final 40 participants from a volunteer\
  \ pool, intentionally correcting demographic skews to mirror the general population's\
  \ mix of age, sex, geographic cantons, political alignments, and education levels.\
  \ To foster an inclusive deliberative environment for laypeople tackling a complex\
  \ technical topic, expert presentations were designed to be factual and accessible.\
  \ Furthermore, facilitators utilized specific group-dynamic exercises on the second\
  \ day\u2014such as \"the power walk\"\u2014to raise awareness about moral equality\
  \ and address potential asymmetries in speaking time, while participants collectively\
  \ established ground rules to guarantee balanced, respectful dialogue."
