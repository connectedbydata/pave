# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "AI-Assisted Deliberation uses large language\
  \ models and big data to support citizens in local decision-making. Grounded in\
  \ transparency and random sampling, the process ensures participants reflect the\
  \ full diversity of a community rather than only those who are most organized or\
  \ vocal. AI tools translate complex policy documents into plain language, draw on\
  \ existing case studies and precedents, and simulate feasibility scenarios so residents\
  \ can weigh real-world trade-offs. During deliberation, real-time prompts and procedural\
  \ reminders help participants engage fairly and reason through options together.\
  \ The result is a process where every resident \u2014 regardless of background or\
  \ prior experience \u2014 has a fair and meaningful role in shaping decisions that\
  \ affect them."

# Why is this project important to include in the case book?
why-is-this-project-important-to-include-in-the: "On inclusion: random sampling deliberately\
  \ reaches residents who rarely engage in formal civic processes \u2014 the elderly,\
  \ lower-income households, people with limited policy literacy, and those skeptical\
  \ of institutions. Policy options are presented using the community's own language,\
  \ anchored in real cases and feasibility analysis, so participation is substantive\
  \ rather than symbolic.\nOn method: rather than using AI to extract or summarize\
  \ public opinion, we use it to actively scaffold the deliberative process itself\
  \ \u2014 surfacing minority views, flagging when discussion drifts from agreed norms,\
  \ and modeling policy implications in real time. Combined with full transparency\
  \ over how inputs are recorded and how outputs are generated, this represents a\
  \ meaningfully different approach to both AI tools and civic participation."
