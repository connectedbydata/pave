# Short description
short-description: Sir John Lazar, Ana Avaliani and Natasha McCarthy discuss the importance
  of the Academy's People's AI Stewardship Summits. The People's AI Stewardship Summits
  are a series of public dialogue events bringing together members of the public with
  experts, innovators, entrepreneurs and other stakeholders to explore hopes, fears
  and expectations of AI to enable positive change and improve people's lives. The
  events are held in partnership with, and co-hosted by, the Academy's Regional Enterprise
  Hubs to create a unique space for stakeholders to listen to local members of the
  communities in which they are based. We held summits across five UK cities Belfast,
  Glasgow, Swansea, Liverpool and London.
