> This is Work in Progress to develop the PAIRS PAVE Case Book. See https://pave.pairs.site for details.
> 
> This is not currently meant for public access / use. 

People across the globe are getting involved in discussions about the opportunities, risks and impacts of AI.

The PAVE Case Book gathers examples of participatory practice on AI, looking for how public engagement can create informed, inclusive and impactful dialogues at community, national and global levels. 

* [DISCOVER](cases) featured case studies of engaement and the messages shared through public voice

* [EXPLORE](map) the map of participatory AI projects and initiatives

* [WATCH](video) a curated video playlist of public engagement projects, and what different communities have to say about AI

* [CONTRIBUTE](contribute) your case studies or upcoming AI-related public engagement projects
